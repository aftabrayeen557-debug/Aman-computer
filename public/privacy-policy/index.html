Privacy Policy

How Aman Computer collects, handles, and safeguards customer information, documentation, and communication across our cyber cafe and digital assistance services.

Privacy Summary

In short: Aman Computer handles customer documents solely for requested form filling, application processing, scanning, or printing. We do not sell your personal information, deploy behavioral ad-tracking cookies, or maintain unauthorized copies of your identity documents.

1. Introduction and Privacy Commitment

At Aman Computer, we recognize that our customers entrust us with sensitive personal details, identification documents, and official applications. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all personal data shared with us, whether in person at our digital center, over WhatsApp, or through this website.

This digital service assistance center and website are operated by Aman Computer (Cyber Cafe & Digital Center), providing citizen documentation facilitation, online form submissions, identity card applications, and digital services.

3. Information Collected From Website Visitors

This website is designed primarily as an informational portal. We collect minimal technical information necessary for providing and displaying our pages:

  • Interface & Theme Preferences: Your browser stores your dark/light mode preference in local storage. This value remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.
  • Server Access Logs: As with standard web servers, hosting providers may temporarily record technical connection data (such as IP addresses, browser user-agent, and request timestamps) solely to maintain network security and defend against abuse.

4. Information Collected During Service Delivery

When you visit Aman Computer or request assistance for specific digital services, you voluntarily provide the details required to process that service, which may include:

  • Contact Details: Name, mobile telephone number, and residential address.
  • Identity Details: Aadhaar number, PAN details, Voter ID number, Driving Licence number, or date of birth as necessary for the specific citizen portal.
  • Academic & Employment Details: Educational marks, certificates, roll numbers, and examination details required for online job applications or entrance exams.
  • Supporting Documents: Passport photos, signature scans, and certificate copies for format conversion and portal upload.

Customers often connect with us via WhatsApp (https://wa.me/918595515718) to check application requirements, verify document eligibility, receive status updates, or send files for printing:

6. Website Analytics & Cookies

To accurately describe our technical implementation:

  • This website does not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.
  • We do not share your browsing habits with ad networks or data brokers.
  • Local storage is used strictly for functional client-side preferences (such as dark mode and local UI state) and does not track you across the web.

7. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We enforce strict data handling hygiene across our workstations:

  • No Unauthorized Copies: Customer identification cards, certificates, photos, and signatures are used strictly to complete the service requested.
  • Workstation Hygiene: Files uploaded to local computers for printing, cropping, resizing, or form filling are routinely cleared and deleted once the task is finished.
  • Customer Acknowledgments: Printed copies, application receipts, challans, and original certificates are handed over directly to the customer.

  • Aman Computer does not control, operate, or maintain government servers or databases.
